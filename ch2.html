Chapter 2

Chapter 2 begins with Kris and Susie returning to school, where they must begin work on a group project. They are invited to study at the library with their classmates Noelle and Berdly, however when they enter the library they notice that it is strangely empty, as they enter the computer lab, it is revealed to now be a Dark World.

This new Dark World is computer themed, as Kris and Susie begin to explore, they meet Noelle who is quickly captured by Queen, the computer ruler of the Cyber World. After a short battle, Ralsei appears and rejoins Kris and Susie, together they continue onward towards the Cyber City and the Dark Fountain.

As they near the entrance to Cyber City, they encounter Queen where she reveals that Berdly has joined her team, and then Kris, Susie, and Ralsei must fight Berdly. After defeating Berdly in a battle, they enter the city, where they must split up, Susie and Ralsei head in one direction, leaving Kris to go on alone.

A Cyber's World

Kris continues on, finding Noelle, who agrees to join Kris, they continue on defeating enemies and completing puzzles. They run into Berdly and must fight him again. After defeating Berdly, Kris and Noelle continue on, eventually meeting back up with Susie and Ralsei in a carnival area. All four continue together, they make to the entrance of Queen's Mansion, where they are captured by Queen and imprisoned inside it.

Kris, Susie and Ralsei rejoin together after escaping their cells, they make their way through Queen's Mansion, clearing puzzles and fighting enemies. They fight and defeat Berdly once more, after this he joins Kris, Susie and Ralsei in their fight against Queen. After fighting Queen and winning, they convince her to give up her goal of opening another fountain, as she didn't know about The Roaring.

Kris seals the Dark Fountain, and they are all transported back to the Light World, Kris and Susie convince Noelle and Berdly that their adventure was just a dream, and they leave. Susie decides to continue hanging out with Kris, together they go to Kris's home, where she decides to stay for a sleepover.
