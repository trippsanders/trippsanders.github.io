Chapter 4

Chapter 4 starts immediately after the end of Chapter 3, Kris and Susie quickly seal the Dark Fountain and are returned to the living room of the Dreemurr household. After this, they explore town and visit Noelle's house, then they attempt to return to Kris's home, but the doors are locked. They go to find Toriel, who is at choir practice at Church, but inside the Church is a Dark World.

This Dark World is the Dark Sanctuary, inside it Kris and Susie immediately find the Dark Fountain, but the Roaring Knight knocks them down into a lower area. After the fall, Ralsei appears and they all continue on together. Inside this Dark World, The Prophecy is displayed in fragments that tell it in more detail than is previously known.

As they make their way through the Dark World, they meet Gerson Boom, an old man who guides them through the Dark World and to his study. They leave Gerson and continue their journey, eventually they make it back to the Dark Fountain, the Roaring Knight attempts to fight them and then leaves. Kris and Susie seal the Dark Fountain and they are transported into a room inside the Church, and inside the other room is another Dark World. But, the door is locked and Kris and Susie must find the code.

Prophecy

They explore the Church and find that Gerson wasn't alive, he was a Darkner reanimation of the real Gerson. Having no other ideas, Susie decides to create another Dark World to ask Gerson the code to the Church door. This new Dark World looks much different and the Darkners are different than the original Dark World. They find Gerson, but something's different, and he attacks Kris and Susie, Ralsei shows up while they're fighting and defeats "Gerson".

Ralsei explains that it was just a statue of Gerson from his study, and that Darkners that are based off a specific person's remains, they only appear in very specific circumstances. After that, they find the code to the door, seal the Dark Fountain, and leave. Back in the Church, they unlock the door and enter the next Dark World. In the next Dark World, they quickly find the Dark Fountain, but the Roaring Knight attacks them while they make their way to the Fountain, eventually they do, and the Knight opens yet another Dark Fountain.

This Dark Fountain inside a Dark World spawns a Titan, a creature of darkness and destruction. Kris, Susie, and Ralsei fight the Titan together, but they can only damage the outer shell. But Susie has an idea, while the Titan's defenses are down, she throws Kris inside it, and Kris seals the Dark Fountain inside the Titan. After defeating the Titan, Kris and Susie go on to the last Fountain and seal it. They are transported back into the Church, Toriel isn't there, but they head back to Kris's home and find Toriel there. Susie leaves, and Kris goes to bed.
