Chapter 1

Deltarune begins with creating a vessel to play as, however you are shortly interrupted by a mysterious voice who states that the vessel will be discarded, because “No one can choose who they are in this world”. You awaken as Kris Dreemurr, a teenage human resident of Hometown, after making your way to school, you meet your teacher, Alphys, and your classmates, including Susie, a reptilian monster and the notorious school bully. You and Susie are sent to retrieve chalk from the closet, but when you enter the closet, it is pitch black, and as you step in further the floor collapses, and together you and Susie fall into a dark void known as The Dark World.

The Dark World

As Kris and Susie make their way through The Dark World, they come upon a castle and met Ralsei, the lonely prince of this world. He tells them about a prophecy, which states that if the balance of Light and Dark isn't maintained, the Earth will be overrun with darkness and be destroyed by an event known as The Roaring. But there are 3 heroes, a human, a monster, and a prince from the dark, who are destined to restore the balance of Light and Dark. Ralsei believes that Kris, Susie, and himself are the heroes, and must begin a quest to seal the Dark Fountains.

As they prepare to depart, they meet Lancer, the son of the King of Spades, ruler of the Card Kingdom, Lancer attempts to stop them but leaves after losing a fight against Kris, Susie, and Ralsei. They enter the Card Kingdom, another Dark World, they must defeat enemies and complete puzzles to progress, they meet Lancer several more times, and eventually Susie decides to join Lancer, because she feels she doesn't belong on the good side. Kris and Ralsei continue without her, and meet Susie and Lancer again, Susie promises to rejoin the heroes if they beat her and Lancer in a fight, they proceed to do so and continue on their way to the Card Castle. As they approach the Card Castle, Lancer ambushes them and captures Kris, Susie, and Ralsei, locking them in the castle dungeon.

The Card Castle

Together, Kris, Susie, and Ralsei manage to escape from the dungeon, by defeating enemies and completing puzzles they make their way through the castle, eventually reaching the King of Spades, the ruler of the Card Kingdom. He will not allow them to seal the Dark Fountain and fights them, during the fight Lancer sees how cruelly King treats the heroes and turns on his father, helping the heroes defeat King and seal the fountain.

After Kris seals the Dark Fountain, Kris and Susie are transported into the second supply closet, they gather up all the contents of this closet, which are the Darkners in the Dark World and bring it into the castle, where the Dark Fountain still flows, which allows them to see Ralsei and their friends from the defunct Card Kingdom.
